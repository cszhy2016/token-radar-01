The model (radar-3)

Implied value = TVL × blended multiple × growth × maturity × chain reach. Every input comes from the sealed snapshot dated (758 protocols, 40 on the watchlist). No protocol name is special-cased.

  • Blended multiple: 75% power-law fit log10(market cap) = a + b·log10(TVL) fitted on the snapshot's live-token protocols, blended in log space with 25% category median market-cap / TVL (same category, at least 3 live protocols; otherwise the overall median). Effective multiple clamped to 0.02–2.00.
  • Fitted values (this snapshot, 128 live-token protocols): log10(market cap) = 3.025 + 0.522·log10(TVL), r = 0.44. The fit is recomputed from the snapshot on every run; nothing is hardcoded.
  • Backtest: leave-one-out on the 128 live-token protocols, median |log10 error| 0.566 vs 0.743 for the category median alone and 0.691 for a flat 0.25×TVL — see PLAN.md.
  • Growth: 7-day TVL change clamped to −60%…+120%, weight 0.5.
  • Maturity: 0.60 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.40; unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Chain reach: 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, capped at 1.10.
